This page summarizes the topics that I work on. Some of the mentioned papers are in-progress: please email me for them. For a chronological list, please consult the [papers](/academic/papers) and [talks](/academic/talks) pages.

{{< collapse title="Representation of morpho-phonological phenomena">}}
- [Morpho-phonology wug-tests and Russian vowel deletion](/academic/papers/scil2026.pdf)\
Underspecification theories of exceptionality are compatible with the results of morpho-phonological wug tests, contrary to earlier claims. Russian vowel deletion is the case study.

- [Cyclicity and exceptionality](/academic/papers/nels2026.pdf) (with Charles Reiss)\
Underspecification theories, when combined with cyclic application of phonological rules, make right predictions for exceptional processes that are also conditioned by morphosyntactic structure.

- [Ms. on derived environment effects](/academic/papers/npa.pdf)\
Underspecification theories, when combined with morpheme structure constraints, generate the full typology of exceptional derived environment-sensitive processes without struggling to generate any individual grammar.

{{< collapse title="Structural locality in allomorphy">}}  

-  [Irish verbal suppletion](/academic/papers/Irish.pdf)\
Fission, combined with non-terminal insertion, may create apparent non-local allomorphy.

-  [Constituent structure in complex words](/academic/papers/complexaffix.pdf)\
If functional heads may form constituents without the root, non-local allomorphy and *ABA violations arise.

-  [Intervention in Terek Kumyk copulas](/academic/papers/kumyk_squib.pdf)\
Vocabulary Insertion has to refer to spans: either as contexts, or as targets.

-  [Allomorphy in Basque xe-comparatives](/academic/papers/wccfl41.pdf)\
Allomorphy in Basque _xe_-comparatives is local, given a richer structure of comparatives, argued for in the Nanosyntax literature.

-  [Generative power of Nanosyntax](/academic/papers/nanoPaper.pdf)\
Basic Nanosyntax generates ABA patterns where all three cells are portmanteau forms.

- [Surface ABA patterns in Russian adjectival morphology](/academic/papers/fdsl2022.pdf)\
Russian adjectival morphology presents ABA patterns that Nanosyntax lexicalization algorithm with subextraction.

{{< collapse title="Structure of cross-categorial derivations">}}  
- [Handout on nominalizations of Russian restructuring verbs](/academic/talks/plc2025.pdf)\
Russian restructuring verbs are unable to take verbal complements, when nominalized. This poses a major problem for the dominant phrasal layering approach to nominalizations.

{{< collapse title="Linearization of syntactic structure">}} 
- [Handout on salvation by deletion in Russian sluicing](/academic/talks/mit_sluicing_May26.pdf) (with Ivan Kalyakin)\
Cyclic linearization overgenerates salvation by deletion configurations observed in Russian left branch extraction. The proposal: Johnson (2020)-inspired two-step model for linearization that provides a new implementation of distributed deletion and a natural account of the restrictions on LBE sluicing.
{{< /collapse >}}

{{< collapse title="Locality of syntactic movement">}} 

- [Subject-object inversion in Russian](/academic/talks/fasl_ovs_poster.pdf) (with Ivan Kalyakin, Maria Berkovich)\
Russian OVS inversion involves demotion of the external argument. The A-minimality puzzle posed by inversion is thus circumvented via Spec-to-Spec anti-locality. We also show that A-minimality obtains for ditransitives: movement of internal argument over the applied argument is impossible if the applied argument raises to subject.

- [Reconstruction in Russian long-distance scrambling](/academic/papers/scrambling.pdf) (with Viktoria Zhukova)\
Russian long-distance scrambling obligatorily reconstructs for quantificational scope. We show that this observation explains why it is unable to feed short wh-movement.

- [Russian _tough_-movement](/academic/papers/tough.pdf)\
Russian _tough_-constructions involve A-movement.

{{< collapse title="Semantics-pragmatics interface" >}} 

+ [Revisionist desires](/academic/papers/desire.pdf)\
Patterns of restricted desires in false-belief contexts requires the question-sensitive formal tools used for revisionist attitudes. Overgeneration worries are tackled by a constraint that only the questions settled in the local context may be used for revision

+ [Russian positive polarity disjunction](/academic/papers/ppi_paper.pdf)\
Russian simplex disjunction shows positive polarity behavior due to competition with simplex conjunction, which shows homogeneity effects. Two cases where the conjunctive alternative ``breaks down'' provide evidence for it.

+ [Semantics restrictions on meta-questions in Russian](/academic/talks/mmq.pdf) (with Petr Rossyaykin)\
Russian employs morphological marking of meta-questions, making it easy to study their restrictions. The current generalization is that MQs cannot target use-conditional content.

+ [Meta-meta-questions in Russian](/academic/papers/metameta.pdf)\
Unlike English, Russian allows meta-meta-questions. I think this is related to availability of wh-scope marking in Russian, if a syntactic analysis of the ban on English MMQs is correct.
